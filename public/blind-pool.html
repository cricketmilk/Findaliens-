BLIND POOL

Venture capital funds the companies that promise to run the future: AI, defense, surveillance, gig work, crypto, space. The money behind it often comes from workers’ pensions, university endowments, Gulf oil states and the CIA. Almost all of it is legal, and almost none of it is visible to the people whose savings and taxes are in the pool.
