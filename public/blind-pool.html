Urbit & Tlon

Urbit is a “personal server” network started by programmer Curtis Yarvin. Tlon, the company built to develop it, was founded in 2013 with early backing from Peter Thiel’s Founders Fund and Andreessen Horowitz. Yarvin is better known under his blogging pen name, Mencius Moldbug, credited with Nick Land as a founder of the “neoreactionary” or “Dark Enlightenment” movement.
