01

THE LEDGER

What the industry knew and when, the campaigns that told the public otherwise, and the bribery, fraud and market abuse cases that reached court. Outright insider-trading convictions tied to climate are rare. Most proven abuses are bribery, misleading investors, and carbon-credit fraud, and they are labelled that way.

02

PRIVATIZED SCIENCE & DATA

Weather and climate data were built with public money and given away free. These entries track attempts to commercialize public forecasting, cut or delete federal climate research, steer university research with corporate endowments, and turn public subsidies, patents and the sky itself into private assets.

03

WHO LOBBIES

The money and organizations working to delay, weaken or block climate policy, with documented examples for each.

04

EMISSIONS TIMELINE

Global CO₂ from fossil fuels and cement at benchmark years, and the CO₂ concentration it built up in the air. More than half of all fossil CO₂ ever emitted has been released since 1990, after the industry’s own scientists had briefed management.

THE TOP 10% FOOTPRINT

Emissions are not shared evenly. These figures assign emissions to people by what they consume and, in most of the inequality studies, by what they own. That is why investments by the very rich count so heavily.

HOW THIS PAGE WAS BUILT

Accusations that have not been proven in court are labelled as such. Where a case was lost or dropped, that is stated too. Every entry was fact-checked against its sources in October 2026. Several cases are still moving, including Suncor v. Boulder at the US Supreme Court and the D.C. Circuit challenge to the endangerment-finding repeal.
