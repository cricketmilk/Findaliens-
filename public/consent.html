Manufacturing Consent

● ON AIRTHE CORNFIELD GAZETTE · MEDIA DESKSIX CHANNELS · ONE SCRIPT

The same few forces run through every story in this project. Paid media tells the public a crash can’t happen, the climate isn’t changing, or a war is necessary. Money builds “grassroots” movements from nothing. And again and again, people’s faith is enlisted to turn them against unions, wages and their own neighbors. These are the documented cases.

How to read the tags

Each case is a specific, sourced event. Together they show recurring incentives: profit, power and the cheapest way to win public opinion. They are not evidence of one hidden hand, and wars and movements have many causes. Interpretation is tagged ARGUMENT.
