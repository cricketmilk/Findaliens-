MANUFACTURINGCONSENT

The same few forces run through every story in this project. Paid media tells the public a crash can’t happen, the climate isn’t changing, or a war is necessary. Money builds “grassroots” movements from nothing. Studios and spy agencies trade script notes for access, and the feed sells paid messages as a friend’s opinion. And again and again, people’s faith is enlisted to turn them against unions, wages and their own neighbors. These are the documented cases.

LINE 01

The Media

How news coverage, advertising and platforms shaped what the public believed about the economy, the climate and technology, often while being paid by the industries involved.

LINE 02

War & Money

Cases where commercial interests, paid PR or false claims helped push countries into war, and who profited.

LINE 03

Bought Movements

“Grassroots” campaigns, think tanks and outrage manufactured or bankrolled by the people who stood to gain, including a whole industry paid to keep workers from organizing.

LINE 04

Faith, Weaponized

Religion has comforted and organized working people for centuries. These cases document employers, industrialists, political operatives and foreign trolls using faith to fight unions, block climate action, cut workers’ benefits, or simply get votes.

LINE 05

Faith With Workers

The same traditions have long stood with labor.

LINE 06

The Dream Factory

Films carry a country’s idea of itself. These cases document the military, the CIA and a foreign government trading access and markets for changes to what audiences saw, and a mogul and a tabloid paying to keep stories from being told.

LINE 07

Paid to Post

The feed looks like people talking. Behind some of it are governments, campaigns, industries and platforms paying for the message, often without saying so. And an old story can be rewritten for it in a weekend.

    ACROSS THIS PROJECT

    Each page documents its own topic in depth. This page tracks how the public was persuaded about them.

    Each case is a specific, sourced event. Together they show recurring incentives: profit, power and the cheapest way to win public opinion. They are not evidence of one hidden hand, and wars and movements have many causes. Interpretation is tagged ARGUMENT. Compiled from court records, government documents, congressional reports, peer-reviewed research and investigative reporting; quotes are reproduced exactly from their sources. Every case was re-checked against its sources in October 2026: each source page was fetched and the passage that states the case’s facts was found on it.
