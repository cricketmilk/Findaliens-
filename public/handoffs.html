THE HANDOFF LEDGER

Public money builds something. The program ends. A private company carries it on. Some of these are documented handoffs: a lab licensed its work, a government sold the asset. Others are only claimed, because a company appeared at about the same time. Every case is graded by the strongest documented link between the two sides, never by timing alone.

05HOW CASES ARE GRADED

  • Each case records the public side from the funder’s own records, the private side from company registries and filings, and each link between them as a separate, sourced item: license, asset transfer, contract, stated successor, personnel, funding, or timing.
  • The grade is calculated from approved links. Grades A and B also require at least two independent publishers. Timing never counts as a link: two dates close together are where an investigation starts, not where it ends.
  • Popular claims are examined separately and assessed as true, partly true, unsupported or false, with the record that decides it.
  • Research is drafted by the Gazette’s research agent and checked against its sources before publication. Something wrong or missing? The sources are listed on every item.
