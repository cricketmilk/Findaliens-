POST-HUMANRESOURCES

Transhumanism promises to upgrade the human body and mind with technology: to end aging, merge with machines, and eventually leave biology behind. This dossier traces where the idea came from, what it includes, who pays for it, and the case that its plans for the future treat working people as a legacy system to be optimized, monitored, or replaced.
