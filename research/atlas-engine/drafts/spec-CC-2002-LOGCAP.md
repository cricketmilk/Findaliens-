## Historical anchors (established facts you may use)
- LOGCAP (Logistics Civil Augmentation Program), launched by the US Army in 1985, pre-awards contingency logistics contracts so contractors support military operations without new wartime procurement.
- Brown & Root (later Halliburton subsidiary KBR; separated as KBR Inc in 2007) won LOGCAP I (1992, Balkans support) and LOGCAP III (December 2001, covering Kuwait, Afghanistan and Iraq).
- LOGCAP III work was awarded on a cost-plus award-fee basis — the fee structure congressional investigators later scrutinised.
- Documented in GAO and DoD-IG reports and congressional hearings: KBR fuel-import pricing (2003–04), dining-facility cost claims (2004–05), and the electrocution deaths of US soldiers in KBR-built showers (DoD-IG 2009).
- Dick Cheney was Halliburton CEO 1995–2000; his deferred compensation from the company continued into his vice presidency — publicly documented, and publicly attacked as a conflict of interest, the framing this node must keep exact.
