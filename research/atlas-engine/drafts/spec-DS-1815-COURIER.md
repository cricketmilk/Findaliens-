## Historical anchors (established facts you may use)
- Nathan Mayer Rothschild: head of the London branch of the family bank, the leading financier of the British war effort against Napoleon after 1813.
- The traditional account: Rothschild had advance news of Napoleons defeat at Waterloo (June 18 1815) via courier or channel, and profited on the London Stock Exchange - the details of the exact channel and the extent of any exclusivity are disputed and were not contemporaneously documented.
- The verifiable core: Rothschilds agents and correspondents gave him faster news of Napoleons return and defeat than the government had, and the Rothschild network financed the post-war adjustment of British government securities.
- After Waterloo the Rothschild brothers (London and Paris) dominated government loan issuance across Europe; their first major operation was financing the French indemnity payment to the Allied powers.
- The courier legend became a durable viral story about insider advantage at the heart of modern finance; the documented record is of a family bank that built a private intelligence network faster than the governments of Europe.
