## Historical anchors (established facts you may use)
- The Marconi scandal (1912-1913): British political scandal over insider dealing in shares of the Marconi Wireless Telegraph Company.
- In March 1912 the British Post Office awarded the Marconi Company a contract for the Imperial wireless chain - the contract was awarded without competitive tender and was criticized in Parliament.
- Postmaster-General Herbert Samuel and other senior Liberal ministers or their associates had purchased Marconi shares before or during the award; the accusation of insider dealing drove a major parliamentary investigation.
- Godfrey Isaacs (Marconi managing director, brother of Rufus Isaacs the Attorney-General who also bought shares) was central; the Isaacs family share purchases were documented by the inquiry.
- The parliamentary inquiry (1913) cleared the ministers of corruption but the scandal damaged the Liberal government; the affair became a durable precedent in British political discourse for the appearance of insider dealing.
