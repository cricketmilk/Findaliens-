## Historical anchors (established facts you may use)
- Operation Ajax (TPAJAX): the CIA/MI6-organized coup of August 19, 1953 that deposed Iranian Prime Minister Mohammad Mossadegh and restored the Shah to full authority.
- Context: the Anglo-Iranian Oil Company refinery at Abadan — Britain's single largest overseas asset — was nationalized by Iran in March 1951; a British embargo and closure of the refinery followed, and Mossadegh moved toward the Soviet bloc as negotiations failed.
- The coup plan used hired crowds, staged disorder and a royal decree dismissing Mossadegh; the first attempt failed on August 16 and the operation succeeded on August 19.
- The CIA's own internal history of the operation, released in part since 2000, and the Agency's 2013 acknowledgment, document the Agency's organizing role: regime change as a remedy for the nationalization of an extraction concession.
