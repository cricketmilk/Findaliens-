## Historical anchors (established facts you may use)
- Operation PBSuccess, the CIA-directed operation that overthrew Guatemala’s elected president Jacobo Árbenz in June 1954, is officially documented in the CIA’s own declassified history (Nick Cullather, 1994).
- Árbenz’s Decree 900 (1952) redistributed unused land, including United Fruit Company holdings, with compensation pegged to the company’s own declared tax valuations — far below market — which became the core dispute.
- United Fruit Company lobbied Washington intensively; the Dulles brothers’ connections to the company (Sullivan & Cromwell had represented UFC, and both brothers held UFC-linked interests) are documented in scholarship.
- The March 1953 arms purchase from Czechoslovakia, and the resulting "communist penetration" framing, supplied the immediate justification used in PBSuccess planning (1952–53).
- Carlos Castillo Armas became president after the coup; UFC lands were largely returned, and decades of civil conflict followed — the ledger outcome the operation purchased.
