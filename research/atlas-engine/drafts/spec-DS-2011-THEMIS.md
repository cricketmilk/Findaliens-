## Historical anchors (established facts you may use)
- Team Themis: a contractor group — Palantir, Berico Technologies and HBGary Federal — assembled late 2010 by the law firm Hunton & Williams (counsel to the U.S. Chamber of Commerce and Bank of America) to propose counter-campaigns against the Chamber's and the bank's perceived opponents.
- The proposals (exposed February 2011 after Anonymous compromised HBGary Federal) suggested infiltration, surveillance and disinformation targeting critics of the Chamber and supporters of WikiLeaks, including named journalists and a union.
- Palantir and Berico severed ties with HBGary; Palantir suspended the employee involved, its CEO apologized publicly, and no contracts were pursued; members of Congress sent letters of inquiry.
- The mechanism the dossier documents: the same data-integration platform sold to the security state proposed, in writing, for domestic counter-surveillance of political opponents — the proposal that surfaced is evidence of the intent, not of any executed operation.
