# Triangulation: trends across the records, and what they do not show

A working note for the owner, written after three research rounds (121
findings files, 42 handoffs). It reads across the records for patterns the
site's pages gesture at, and says plainly where the corpus has nothing. The
records it cites are drafts until approved with `node research/review.mjs`.

## Trends the records document

**Insider timing is real but narrower than the pages say.** Where the record
is primary, the timing fact survives and the "insiders knew" gloss does not.
The IMF's own history says Mexican capital flight came *after* the December
20, 1994 announcement, probably led by people warned at the Pacto meeting the
night before (`fobaproa`). The Bank of New York flows ran 1996 to 1999, not
"just before" the 1998 default (`bank-of-new-york`). SIGTARP found Geithner
did not take part in the AIG counterparty negotiations (`maiden-lane`). The
one timing episode that stands as documented, Paulson's July 2008 meeting
with hedge-fund managers, is a node with no record yet (round four).

**Statements that the record later contradicts are the best-sourced trend.**
The Global Climate Coalition's 1995 primer ("cannot be denied") against its
public backgrounder; Shell's 1988 report against a 16-year silence to
shareholders; Total's 1971 forecast; the Edison Electric Institute's 1991
campaign to "reposition global warming as theory"; Luntz's memo and his 2019
"I was wrong"; the Senate's finding that the Bank of England withheld BCCI
information from the Fed; DOJ's amended release softening "admitted its
criminal conduct" on BoNY; the Paris court's 2025 finding that TotalEnergies'
consumer statements were misleading. All court-, filing- or inquiry-grade.

**Manufactured consent for war is documented once, cleanly.** Kuwait's
government paid $11.9M to Citizens for a Free Kuwait, almost all of it to
Hill & Knowlton, which coached the ambassador's daughter before the Nayirah
testimony (1990, `hill-and-knowlton`). The 2003 instance is in the corpus
only as Halliburton's no-bid Restore Iraqi Oil contract, "coordinated" with
the Vice President's office per an Army Corps email, and the Waxman figures
(`halliburton`). The intelligence findings on the Iraq case are round four.

**Gulf sovereign money recurs as capital, each time sourced.** PIF's $45B
into the Vision Fund and Mubadala's $15B (`softbank`); Humain's $3B into
xAI and its Nvidia chips (`xai`, `nvidia`); Abu Dhabi's 77% of BCCI; Kamal
Adham as Saudi intelligence chief, the CIA's Middle East liaison and BCCI's
front man in the First American takeover (`kamal-adham`, Senate report).
The same actors cycle through finance, intelligence and technology. That is
a pattern of capital, not a finding of conspiracy, and the records keep the
two apart.

**The private-benefit-from-public-programme pattern holds where the ledger
grades it A**, and the "trivial cost" framing fails every time it is tested
(C2S: false; Keyhole and Moderna: partly true; COTS "sole-source": false).

## Operation Ajax

In the records twice, from the CIA's own 2013 release (`bp`, `cia`): the
1951 nationalization, the August 1953 coup, the 1954 consortium that gave
BP's predecessor 40%. Nothing in the corpus runs forward from 1954. The
sourced chain to build is: consortium → 1979 → the Iran-Contra arms
channels as the Walsh report and the Senate BCCI chapters describe them →
sanctions → the 2015 JCPOA and the 2018 withdrawal → the IAEA's 2025 reports
→ the June 2025 strikes and the dispute between the administration's
statements and the DIA's initial assessment → the Senate war-powers vote.
Round four writes `iran.json` along that chain. Whether the 2025 strikes were
warranted is not something the records can decide; what they can hold is
what the IAEA, the DNI, the DoD and the DIA each said, dated.

## 9/11, Saudi Arabia and Iraq

The site has two nodes (`nine_eleven_2001`, `wtc7_put_options_2001`) with
no citations, and the corpus has nothing on the attacks. The documented
starting points, all round-four targets: the 9/11 Commission's finding of
"no evidence that the Saudi government as an institution or senior Saudi
officials individually funded" al-Qaeda; the 28 pages released in 2016; the
FBI's Operation Encore releases on al-Bayoumi and Thumairy; the families'
suit in SDNY and its latest ruling; the Carlyle Group's bin Laden family
limited partnership (acknowledged by Carlyle; the record has Carlucci, Bush
and Baker but not that). On Iraq: the Senate Intelligence Committee's 2008
report and the Chilcot report are the official findings that the WMD case
was presented with a certainty the intelligence did not justify. The records
do not support "it was Saudi Arabia" as a conclusion. They will support
"the institutional findings and the released documents disagree, and here
is each one" as the honest page.

## Suspicious timing worth a deep dive, by how much primary record exists

1. Pre-9/11 put options: the Commission's note 130 and the SEC review say
   the trades traced to a US investor with no conceivable ties; Poteshman's
   2006 paper found the volumes unusual. Both sides exist.
2. October 7 short-selling: Jacobs and Mitts (2023) against the Israel
   Securities Authority's and Leumi's responses on units and attribution.
3. The January 2020 Senate stock sales: DOJ closed the Burr inquiry in 2020,
   the SEC in 2023; Senate Ethics dismissed Loeffler, Feinstein and Inhofe.
4. Paulson and Eton Park: Bloomberg's 2011 account, Paulson's response, and
   no documented trade on the information.

All four are round-four targets (`henry-paulson`, `richard-burr`, `sec`,
`israel-securities-authority`).

## Epstein

The site's node (`epstein_blackmail_carbyne`) treats him as a blackmail
vault. The owner's hypothesis for round four is narrower and testable: a
financier who absorbed others' lost money and moved it into crypto. The
record to test it against: the Towers Financial Ponzi (SEC 1993, the
Hoffenberg plea, Hoffenberg's later statements and suit about Epstein's
role), Wexner's power of attorney, Leon Black's fees per the Senate Finance
Committee, the NYDFS Deutsche Bank order and the JPMorgan settlements, and
whatever the 2025 estate releases say about crypto. Round four writes
`jeffrey-epstein`, `towers-financial`, `steven-hoffenberg` and `leon-black`.

## What should not go on a page

The records support trends; they do not support the specific accusations
the pages make in their own voice. The safe shape, which the Crash Conduits
page now has, is: editorial node, then "On The Web" showing which of its
names carry approved sourced items, then the reader decides.
